I am a final-year Computer Science undergraduate at Nanyang Technological University (NTU) in Singapore (First Class Honours, GPA 4.84 / 5.00) with proven expertise in low-level systems, algorithms, and software engineering.

Currently, I work as a Machine Learning Engineer at Huawei, where I build computer-use autonomous agents for OpenJiuwen, focusing on multi-modal reasoning, alignment, memory management, and agentic evaluation harnesses. Previously at Huawei Nanjing Research Center and Singapore, I built PyPTO, a Python framework targeting Huawei Ascend NPUs to optimize developer ergonomics, runtime efficiency, and numerical accuracy. Prior to that, I engineered production ML pipelines leveraging LLMs, VLMs, and speculative decoding at ST Engineering.

I have an extensive international competitive programming background, having earned two Silver Medals at the International Olympiad in Informatics (IOI), an ICPC Regional Gold Medal, and an ICPC Asia Pacific Championships Bronze Medal. I was also the winner of Ruangguru Clash of Champions II and runner-up at the Road of Champions.

I am seeking full-time software engineering and machine learning opportunities in Singapore starting May 2027. Feel free to reach out via email or connect on LinkedIn.
