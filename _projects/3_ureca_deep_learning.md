### NTU Undergraduate Research Experience on Campus (URECA, 2025)

Conducted under NTU's premier undergraduate research framework, this project investigated learning-based heuristics for complex combinatorial optimization and decision problems.

#### Overview & Highlights
- **AlphaGo-style Self-Play:** Designed and implemented deep learning agents trained through self-play and Monte Carlo tree search mechanisms inspired by AlphaGo.
- **Optimization Heuristics:** Evaluated agent performance on classic NP-hard optimization and sequential decision benchmarks.
- **Empirical Analysis:** Benchmarked algorithmic convergence, computational efficiency, and generalization bounds against traditional metaheuristics and integer programming solvers.
