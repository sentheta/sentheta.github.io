### TOKI Regular Open Contest 31 (2023)

Authored and coordinated competitive programming problems for **TOKI Regular Open Contest 31 (TROC 31)**, hosted on the TLX platform and sponsored by the **official Indonesian IOI Training Team (TOKI)**.

#### Contributions
- **Original Algorithmic Problem Creation:** Developed original, challenging algorithmic and combinatorial problems covering topics in graph theory, dynamic programming, number theory, and advanced data structures.
- **Testcase Generation & Validation:** Developed rigorous test generators, comprehensive corner-case suites, and automated solution validators in C++ and Python.
- **Contest Operations & Editorials:** Authored full pedagogical editorials detailing optimal time and space complexity analyses, alternative approaches, and implementation details for hundreds of participating international contestants.
